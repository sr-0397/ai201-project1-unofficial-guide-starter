Housing plays a major role in the freshman experience at the University of Georgia. Many first-year students live in traditional residence halls where they share rooms, bathrooms, and common spaces with other students. Residence halls such as Russell, Brumby, and Creswell create opportunities for social interaction and community building. Students often meet friends through floor activities, residence hall events, study sessions, and casual conversations in lounges. Living on campus also provides convenient access to dining facilities, libraries, classrooms, and student organizations. Freshman housing is designed to help students transition from high school to university life by encouraging involvement and independence. While individual experiences vary, many students report that residence hall communities become an important part of their first year. Shared living environments help students develop communication skills, build friendships, and learn how to manage daily responsibilities. Residence halls remain one of the primary ways that new students connect with the larger university community.
