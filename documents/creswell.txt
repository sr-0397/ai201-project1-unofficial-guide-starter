Creswell Hall is one of the largest freshman residence halls at the University of Georgia. Located on Baxter Street, it forms the third member of the freshman high-rise community along with Russell and Brumby. Creswell houses more than nine hundred first-year students and is known for its energetic and highly social atmosphere. Students frequently encounter one another in hallways, lounges, elevators, and the busy lobby area. The residence hall is positioned close to Bolton Dining Commons, making meals convenient for residents. One advantage frequently mentioned by students is Creswell's location near central academic buildings, including the Miller Learning Center and Terry College areas. While Creswell is often viewed as older than Russell or Brumby, many students appreciate its strong sense of community and opportunities to make friends. The building primarily offers traditional double rooms with community bathrooms. Over the years, student opinions about Creswell have varied, with some preferring newer halls and others valuing its social culture. For many freshmen, Creswell serves as an introduction to campus life and provides easy access to dining, classes, and student organizations throughout the academic year.
