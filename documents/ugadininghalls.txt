The University of Georgia operates multiple dining commons that serve students across campus. Major dining locations include Bolton Dining Commons, Oglethorpe House Dining Commons, Snelling Dining Commons, and Village Summit. Together, these facilities provide students with access to a wide range of meal options throughout the academic year. Dining commons typically offer buffet-style service and allow students to choose from multiple stations featuring different cuisines. Menu options often include salads, sandwiches, grilled foods, international cuisine, vegetarian dishes, desserts, and allergen-conscious selections. Students can use meal plans, Paw Points, and other payment methods at dining facilities. The university emphasizes convenience by locating dining halls near major residential communities. Dining commons are important gathering places where students study, socialize, and meet friends between classes. Because food quality and variety are significant factors in student satisfaction, UGA continuously updates menus and dining offerings to meet student preferences. The dining system is widely regarded as one of the strengths of student life at the university.
