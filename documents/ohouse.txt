Oglethorpe House Dining Commons, commonly called "O-House" by students, is a popular dining hall located on the east side of the University of Georgia campus. Many students consider O-House one of the best dining locations because of its food quality, convenient layout, and comfortable atmosphere. The dining hall serves a variety of meals throughout the day and attracts students from nearby residence halls and academic buildings. O-House has developed a strong reputation among students, and many upperclassmen continue to visit it even after moving off campus. The dining commons often experiences heavy traffic during lunch and dinner hours due to its popularity. Students appreciate the dining hall's consistent food quality and welcoming environment. Online student discussions frequently highlight positive experiences with O-House staff and dining operations. Because it is located on East Campus, it serves as an important dining option for students living in nearby residence halls and apartments. O-House helps distribute dining traffic across campus and provides students with another high-quality alternative to larger dining commons such as Bolton.
