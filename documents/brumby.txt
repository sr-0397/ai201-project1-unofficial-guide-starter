Brumby Hall is a large freshman residence hall located on Baxter Street at the University of Georgia. It is one of the three famous freshman high-rises and houses more than nine hundred students. Brumby is known for its strong social environment and extensive study spaces. Students often describe Brumby as one of the most active freshman communities on campus because so many first-year students live there. The building contains lounges, study rooms, laundry facilities, and common areas that encourage interaction among residents. Although Brumby was originally built in the 1960s, it has received renovations that make it more modern than many older residence halls. Community bathrooms are shared by students on each floor, and residents frequently mention that custodial staff keep them relatively clean. The hall's location near Bolton Dining Commons is another major advantage. Students can easily walk to dining, classes, and campus events. While some students complain about the steep hill leading to the building, many believe the social opportunities and facilities outweigh the inconvenience. Brumby remains one of the most sought-after residence halls for freshmen who want a traditional and highly social college experience.
