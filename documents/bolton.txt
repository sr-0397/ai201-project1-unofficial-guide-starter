Bolton Dining Commons is one of the most popular dining locations at the University of Georgia and is the largest dining commons on campus. Located near the freshman high-rise residence halls, Bolton serves thousands of students each week. The facility offers a wide variety of food options throughout the day, including breakfast, lunch, dinner, and late dining. Students can choose from burgers, sandwiches, tacos, salads, pasta, stir-fry, desserts, and rotating specialty dishes. Popular stations include The Wok, Tanyard Grill, Taqueria, and Buon Appetito. Bolton is especially convenient for residents of Russell, Brumby, and Creswell Halls because it is within a short walking distance. The dining commons is also open seven days a week, making it a dependable option for students with varying schedules. Many students appreciate the large seating areas and the extensive variety of food available. Bolton also provides allergen-friendly and gluten-friendly meal options through its Special Selections station. Because of its location, food variety, and capacity, Bolton remains one of the busiest and most recognizable dining facilities at the University of Georgia.
