Russell Hall, Brumby Hall, and Creswell Hall are collectively known as the freshman high-rises at the University of Georgia. These three residence halls house thousands of first-year students and play a major role in shaping the freshman experience. Russell is generally viewed as the most modern due to its extensive renovation, while Brumby is praised for its study spaces and active social environment. Creswell is often considered the oldest of the three, but many students value its strong sense of community and central location. All three halls use traditional room layouts with community bathrooms. Their proximity to Bolton Dining Commons makes dining convenient for residents. Students choosing among the three often prioritize factors such as room condition, social atmosphere, study spaces, and location. Russell and Brumby frequently receive praise for their updated facilities, while Creswell is recognized for being highly social and close to important academic buildings. Despite their differences, all three halls provide a classic freshman living experience and offer opportunities to meet other students and become involved in campus life.
