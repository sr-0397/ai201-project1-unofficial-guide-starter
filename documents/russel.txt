Russell Hall is one of the most popular freshman residence halls at the University of Georgia. Located on Baxter Street, it is part of the group commonly known as the freshman high-rises alongside Brumby Hall and Creswell Hall. Russell houses nearly one thousand first-year students and is known for combining a strong social atmosphere with relatively modern facilities. The building underwent a major renovation and reopened in 2018, giving students updated rooms, study spaces, lounges, and community areas. Students often choose Russell because it balances convenience and social opportunities. The hall is within walking distance of Bolton Dining Commons, Tate Student Center, the Miller Learning Center, and many academic buildings. Community bathrooms are shared by residents on each floor, but many students report that the facilities are cleaner and more private than those found in older residence halls. Russell is considered one of the most desirable freshman dorms due to its location, renovated interior, and active student community. Many incoming students rank Russell as their top housing choice because it offers the traditional freshman experience while still providing modern amenities and comfortable common spaces.
